- Real-time text messaging
- Share photos, videos, and files
- Voice messages
- Emoji reactions
- Starred and pinned messages

VOICE AND VIDEO CALLS
- HD voice calls
- High-quality video calls
- Works on mobile data or WiFi

GROUPS

MEXIVANZA COMMUNITY
